# Spend Approval and Compliance Policy

## Spend Categories
Recognized spend categories are: Travel, Software, Hardware, Consulting,
Marketing, Facilities, and Training. Every transaction must be assigned to
exactly one category.

## Budget Ownership
Each cost center owns its annual budget. Spend that would exceed 90% of the
annual category budget triggers a review by the finance business partner.

## Anomaly Review
Finance runs a monthly anomaly review. Any monthly category total that deviates
more than two standard deviations from its trailing average is flagged for
investigation and requires a written explanation from the budget owner.

## Segregation of Duties
The person who raises a purchase order cannot be the same person who approves
the corresponding invoice for payment.
