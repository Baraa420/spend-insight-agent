# Travel and Expense Policy

## Flights
Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is permitted only
for intercontinental flights longer than 8 hours, and requires manager approval
in advance. First class travel is not reimbursable.

## Accommodation
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 200 EUR per night in standard locations and up
to 300 EUR per night in high-cost cities. Room service and minibar charges are
not reimbursable.

## Meals and Per Diem
A per diem of 60 EUR per day covers meals during business travel. Alcohol is not
reimbursable. Client-entertainment meals must be itemized and pre-approved.

## Expense Submission
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip with itemized
receipts. Reports submitted after 60 days may be rejected.
