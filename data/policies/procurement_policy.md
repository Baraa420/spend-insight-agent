# Procurement Policy

## Purchase Order Approval Limits
A single purchase order (PO) up to 5,000 EUR may be approved by a team lead.
A PO between 5,000 and 50,000 EUR requires cost-center manager approval.
Any PO above 50,000 EUR requires director-level approval and a competitive bid
from at least three vendors.

## Preferred Vendors
Spend should be directed to preferred vendors where a framework agreement exists.
Using a non-preferred vendor for spend above 10,000 EUR requires a documented
justification and procurement team sign-off.

## Purchase Order Process
All purchases must be raised as a PO before the goods or services are received.
Retroactive POs are not permitted except for documented emergencies approved by
a cost-center manager.
